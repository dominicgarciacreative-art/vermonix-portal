How this is calculated

Gross sell-in is what you invoiced from Vermonix in the period. Your margin is applied at the contracted rate on the recommended retail price, net of the trade discount already taken at invoice. Co-op marketing is the agreed contribution to campaign assets and testers, deducted at 1.5% of gross. Net to me is what lands in your account on the payment date, subject to your trading terms.
